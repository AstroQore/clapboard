Installed

Clapboard Bus 1 … Clapboard Bus 4 should now be listed in System Settings › Sound and in Audio MIDI Setup.

If they are not there yet, restart Core Audio once more from Terminal:

sudo killall coreaudiod

To remove the devices later, run the Clapboard driver uninstaller package, or ./Scripts/uninstall_driver.sh from the Clapboard source tree.
