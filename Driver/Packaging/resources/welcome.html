Clapboard Virtual Audio Devices

This installs ClapboardDriver.driver into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL and adds four loopback audio devices to macOS: Clapboard Bus 1 through Clapboard Bus 4.

Each bus is a 2-channel, 48 kHz virtual device. Anything an app plays to a bus can be captured from the same bus by any other app — OBS, Zoom, Audio Hijack, QuickTime — with no cables and no extra software.

  • The installer needs an administrator password: the plug-in folder is owned by the system.
  • Core Audio is restarted at the end of the install, so audio drops out for a second and running audio apps may need to be restarted.
  • Nothing else on the system is touched, and no background agent is added.
